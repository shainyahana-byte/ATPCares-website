About the App

Built around three core pillars

Every feature in ATPCares was designed with one goal: making it simple and transparent to support children's homes in Anantapur.

Verified Directory

Aggregating local orphanages and homes in one place, transparently listing their urgent real-time needs so donors know exactly where help is required.

Mentorship Hub

Allowing volunteers to discover open tutoring and skill-sharing slots, connecting their time and knowledge directly with children who need guidance.

Tech Stack

Built with Flutter for smooth cross-platform performance on both Android and iOS, powered by a robust Supabase backend for real-time data and authentication.
