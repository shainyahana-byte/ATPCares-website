About the App

Built around three core pillars

Every feature in ATPCares was designed with one goal: making it simple and transparent to support children's homes in Anantapur.

Verified Directory

Only vetted children's homes and shelters are published, each with resident counts, contact details, and the exact items it needs, so donors know where help is required.

Meals, Visits & Offers

Citizens sponsor breakfast, lunch, or dinner slots, book calm one-party-per-slot visits, and offer spare items that shelters can request directly.

Tech Stack

A Flutter and Dart client backed by Supabase: PostgreSQL, Realtime WebSockets, Storage, and Row Level Security keep wishlists and meal slots in sync across devices.

Functional Modules

Six modules. One coherent system.

01

Verified Homes & Wishlists

A shelter directory with demographic breakdowns, plus targeted item needs with required quantities and live progress meters.

02

Smart Meal Sponsorship

Per-head veg & non-veg platters, configurable add-ons, and real-time slot locking across breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

03

Citizen Donation Exchange

Peer-to-shelter gifting: citizens post surplus items, shelters claim what they need, donors approve allocations.

04

Community Visits

Slot-based scheduling caps attendance at one party per window, with automated check-in to verify visits.

05

Proof of Work

Activity galleries with client-side image compression and aspect-ratio cropping showcase real shelter outcomes.

06

Direct Financial Channels

Shelters configure official UPI identifiers for transparent, fee-free direct contributions.

Meal Pulse

No duplicate meals. Ever.

Citizens sponsor specific meal slots — breakfast, lunch, or dinner. Once booked, slots lock in real time to prevent duplicate sponsorships. Veg and non-veg platters are priced per head, with add-ons like juice or halwa.
