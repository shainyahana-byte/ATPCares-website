Platform Capabilities

Everything needed to support local shelters

Making it simple, transparent, and direct to support verified children's homes in Anantapur.

Verified Wishlists

Vetted directory featuring resident counts, shelter details, and live fulfillment meters for targeted item requests.

Meals & Visits

Real-time slot locking for meal sponsorships, transparent per-head menus, and scheduled one-party visit windows.

Direct Giving

Citizen surplus item exchange, direct zero-fee shelter UPI contributions, and photo-verified proof of impact.

Modern Stack

Cross-platform Flutter frontend backed by Supabase with PostgreSQL, Row Level Security, and Realtime sync.

Real-Time Wishlists

The live need gallery

Shelters list specific essentials with required quantities and commitment counters. Donors pledge the exact items — never unneeded goods.

Sample wishlist entries shown for illustration.

Meal Pulse

No duplicate meals. Ever.

Citizens sponsor specific meal slots — breakfast, lunch, or dinner. Once booked, slots lock in real time to prevent duplicate sponsorships. Veg and non-veg platters are priced per head, with add-ons like juice or halwa.

Hover the density key to feel the community pulse.
